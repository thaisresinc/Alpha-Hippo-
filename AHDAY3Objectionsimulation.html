SANDRA
You know what, it all sounds great honestly. The bills have been killing us. But look — I'd love to, we just can't afford it right now.
YOUR MOVE — STEP D
What's your first response to Sandra?
"Have you heard about how you can install solar for nothing upfront? The savings actually help pay off the system…"
"I'm really glad you brought that up Sandra — that's actually one of the most important things to understand."
"Don't worry about cost — that's something the specialist will go through in detail with you."
YOUR MOVE — STEP T
Sandra's guard is lower. What's your transition?
"That's actually one of the most common misconceptions about going solar…"
"But actually, solar is quite affordable for most families like yours these days…"
"I understand — let me walk you through our PAYS program in a bit more detail…"
YOUR MOVE — STEP R
D and T done. Which response do you deliver?
"There is a cost for the system — but you can pay it upfront, or set up a plan that fits your budget instead."
"Have you heard how you can install solar for no upfront cost? As your bill drops, the savings cover the repayments — sometimes they even exceed them."
"The government also gives you thousands of dollars back in rebates — so most people end up paying very little out of pocket."
YOUR MOVE — STEP C
You've delivered the PAYS response. How do you check in?
"So — would you like to go ahead and book in an assessment time that suits you, Sandra?"
"Based on that — if we could design a system with no upfront cost, would you be opposed to seeing how much we could save you?"
"Does that all make sense so far, Sandra, or is there anything you'd like me to run through again?"

MARCUS
Yeah look, I'm keen. It's just — my wife works nights, she's barely home when I'm awake. Getting us both in the same room at the same time is a nightmare. Can't we just do it with me?
YOUR MOVE — STEP D
Marcus wants to proceed without his wife. How do you open?
"Look Marcus, we really do need both of you there for the assessment — that's just our policy."
"I completely understand — it can be tricky getting everyone in the same place with different schedules."
"No problem — we can run the assessment with just you, and I'll pass the details to your wife afterwards."
YOUR MOVE — STEP T
You've disarmed. Now reframe why it matters.
"The thing is Marcus — solar is a big decision and a structural change to the property. We genuinely want you both on the same page."
"It's really not a big deal — but technically, she does still need to sign off on the paperwork too."
"Actually, most couples find that one person makes the call and just explains the details afterwards."
YOUR MOVE — STEP R
Deliver the both-party response. Which one?
"The assessment itself is free, so there's no reason we couldn't do it separately — we can always book a quick follow-up with your wife."
"We're custom-designing the system based on everyone's usage — having you both there means the highest possible return, and keeps the assessment complimentary."
"Your wife could review everything we go through and make the decision later — we'll leave all the documentation behind for her."
YOUR MOVE — STEP C
Response delivered. Move Marcus forward.
"So — when do you and your wife both think you could be free to sit down together and go through it properly?"
"Does that all make sense as to why we'd really love to have both of you there together?"
"Would you be opposed to finding a time that works for both of you — even if it's a few weeks out? We want you both to get the most from it."
