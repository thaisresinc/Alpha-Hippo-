◦ THE THREE THAT COST CALLS ◦
✓Filling the silence. A setter last week said "I think you guys were a little bit busy at the time." She never confirmed it — he'd invented a reason, so he had no diagnosis and booked on goodwill and nothing else.
✓Booking too early. Fresh leads are warm and often say yes in the first minute. Take it, but keep working — book before the bill, the pain and the pitch and you've got an appointment with no value built.
✓Rushing the pause. Aged only. Talk over her processing and she never places the conversation, so everything after is a stranger asking about her power bill.
◦ WORTH KEEPING ON THE BOARD ◦
·Say "energy assessment", never "solar". Fresh leads say no to solar. Nobody says no to an assessment they already agreed to.
·The slip is the door rep's version. If she contradicts it, she's right and the file's wrong — don't argue the note.
·The shed and the roof belong in the close, not the opener. That's your way in later.
